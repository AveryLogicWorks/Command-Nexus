# Command Nexus™ — Demo Video Script
**For YouTube & Landing Page Video**
**Estimated runtime: 3–5 minutes**

---

## Setup Notes
- Record at 1080p or 1440p, 60fps
- Use OBS Studio or similar screen recorder
- Have Command Nexus open and ready with a pre-built AI agent
- Keep the avatar visible during chat portions
- Speak naturally — you're a builder talking to another builder, not a corporate narrator

---

## SCRIPT

### [0:00 — Cold Open: The Hook]

**VISUAL:** Screen is dark. Text fades in: *"You don't use AI. You command it."*

**NARRATOR:**
> Everyone's talking about AI. ChatGPT. Claude. AutoGPT. They all do the same thing — they let you *talk* to an AI.
>
> But what if you didn't want to talk? What if you wanted to *command*?
>
> This is Command Nexus. The desktop mission control for your AI army.

---

### [0:20 — Title Card & Hero Reveal]

**VISUAL:** Command Nexus launches. The main window appears — dark theme, 5 tabs visible: Visibility, Forge, Book, Upgrades, Watcher. The 3D avatar is idle, gently breathing.

**NARRATOR:**
> Command Nexus runs on your machine. Not in a browser. Not in the cloud. On *your* hardware. Your data never leaves unless you say so.

---

### [0:35 — Part 1: AI Forge — Build an AI in 60 Seconds]

**VISUAL:** Click the **Forge** tab. Show the character-sheet builder.

**NARRATOR:**
> First, you build your AI. No code. No prompt engineering. Just a character sheet.
>
> Give it a name — let's call this one "Scout." Pick a use-case class — we'll go with Task-Ready. Now choose capabilities — Research, Coder, Tool User. Slide the personality dials — high creativity, medium caution.
>
> Click "Save AI to Forge." Done. You just built a custom AI agent in under a minute.

**VISUAL:** Click "Save AI to Forge." The AI appears in the library list.

---

### [1:15 — Part 2: The Book — Teach It What to Remember]

**VISUAL:** Select "Scout" in the library. Click "Open Book for AI."

**NARRATOR:**
> Now teach it what to remember. Every AI gets its own Book — not a shared brain, a private compendium.
>
> Click "Talk to Book Keeper." It asks you seven questions: What is this AI for? Who will it help? What should it always follow? What should it always remember? What's the current context? Your preferences? Its hard boundaries?
>
> Answer in plain English. The Book Keeper writes the governance document for you.

**VISUAL:** Walk through the Book Keeper dialog — answer 2-3 questions to show the flow. Click save. Show the tree structure: Parts → Chapters → Sections.

**NARRATOR:**
> You can edit the tree manually later. Roll back to defaults anytime. Your AI's memory is a Book — and you're the author.

---

### [2:00 — Part 3: Visibility Window — Deploy & Command]

**VISUAL:** Click "Deploy to Command Center." Switch to the **Visibility Window** tab.

**NARRATOR:**
> Scout is now in the Command Center. You can see the live viewport, the audit panes — Thought, Action, Planned Trajectory — and the mission control bar.
>
> Let's give Scout a mission.

**VISUAL:** Type a task in the mission input: "Research the top 5 Python web frameworks and summarize their pros and cons." Click START.

**NARRATOR:**
> Hit START. Scout begins working. Watch the audit panes — you see what it's thinking, what it's doing, and where it's going next. In real time.
>
> If Scout tries something risky — writing a file, running a shell command — an approval gate pops up. You see what, why, and what it affects. You approve or deny. You're still the general.

**VISUAL:** Show the audit panes updating. If possible, trigger an approval gate to show the modal dialog. Click Approve.

**VISUAL:** Hover over a few modules. Toggle one from Lite to Pro. Show the resource bar reacting.

## Recording Tips
- Do a dry run first — make sure the AI mission produces visible audit pane activity
- Pre-build the "Scout" AI so you don't have to fill the character sheet live (or do it live — it's fast)
- If the avatar video assets are available, have them loaded so the talking state is visible
- Keep energy up — punchy, confident, slightly rebellious tone per the brand guidelines
- No "um"s or "uh"s — do multiple takes if needed
- Record audio separately for clean voiceover if screen capture audio is noisy
