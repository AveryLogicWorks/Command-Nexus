## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules and guidelines for using Command Nexus™ ("the Software") and all AI Agents created through the Software. This AUP is incorporated by reference into our Terms of Service.

**By using Command Nexus, you agree to comply with this AUP.** Violation of this AUP may result in immediate suspension or termination of your account, voidance of your license, and potential legal action.

## 2. PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

### 2.1 ILLEGAL ACTIVITIES

You may NOT use the Software or any AI Agent to:
- Violate any applicable local, state, national, or international law, statute, ordinance, or regulation
- Facilitate, promote, or engage in any illegal activity, including but not limited to:
  - Fraud, financial crimes, or money laundering
  - Terrorism or violent extremism
  - Human trafficking or exploitation
  - Drug trafficking or illegal substance distribution
  - Cybercrime, hacking, or unauthorized system intrusion
- Generate, store, or distribute content that is illegal in any jurisdiction where you operate or where the content may be accessed
- Evade or circumvent export controls, sanctions, or trade restrictions

### 2.2 HARMFUL AND DANGEROUS CONTENT

You may NOT use the Software or any AI Agent to create, generate, distribute, or promote:

**Violence and Physical Harm:**
- Content that promotes, encourages, or provides instructions for violence, terrorism, or physical harm to individuals or groups
- Content that glorifies or encourages suicide, self-harm, or eating disorders
- Instructions for creating weapons, explosives, dangerous chemicals, or harmful devices
- Content that facilitates stalking, harassment, or physical intimidation

**Hate Speech and Discrimination:**
- Content that promotes hatred, discrimination, or violence against individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic
- Content that promotes extremist ideologies, hate groups, or discriminatory movements
- Targeted harassment or bullying of individuals or groups

**Sexual Content and Exploitation:**
- Sexually explicit content, pornography, or content created for sexual gratification
- Non-consensual intimate imagery ("revenge porn")
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any content sexualizing minors
- Content that facilitates sexual exploitation, human trafficking, or commercial sexual services

### 2.3 DECEPTION, FRAUD, AND MISINFORMATION

You may NOT use the Software or any AI Agent to:

**Impersonation and Identity Fraud:**
- Impersonate real individuals (living or deceased) without explicit written consent and disclosure
- Create "deepfakes" or synthetic media intended to deceive viewers about the identity of speakers
- Generate fake identities, credentials, or documentation for deceptive purposes
- Impersonate government officials, law enforcement, or representatives of organizations

**Fraud and Scams:**
- Create phishing content, social engineering materials, or fraudulent communications
- Generate content for fake investment schemes, Ponzi schemes, or financial fraud
- Create fake reviews, testimonials, or endorsements
- Generate counterfeit currency, securities, or official documents

**Misinformation and Disinformation:**
- Generate fake news, propaganda, or disinformation campaigns intended to deceive
- Create misleading content about elections, democratic processes, or civic matters
- Generate health misinformation that could cause harm (e.g., anti-vaccine conspiracies, dangerous "cures")
- Create conspiracy theories or content designed to undermine public trust in institutions

**Academic and Professional Dishonesty:**
- Generate content for plagiarism, cheating on academic assignments, or credential fraud
- Create fake research papers, data, or scientific findings
- Generate content to circumvent academic integrity policies

### 2.4 HIGH-RISK ACTIVITIES WITHOUT APPROPRIATE SAFEGUARDS

You may NOT use the Software or any AI Agent for the following high-risk purposes **without implementing appropriate human oversight, review, and safeguards**:

**Medical and Health:**
- Providing medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or health advice without appropriate disclaimers and human medical professional review
- Interpreting medical tests, imaging, or diagnostic results
- Recommending medications, dosages, or treatment protocols
- Providing mental health counseling or crisis intervention without proper qualifications

**Legal and Financial:**
- Providing legal advice, interpretation of laws, or legal strategy without being a qualified attorney
- Drafting legally binding contracts, wills, or legal documents without attorney review
- Providing financial, investment, or tax advice without appropriate licenses and disclaimers
- Making credit decisions, loan approvals, or insurance underwriting decisions
- Assessing criminal risk or predicting criminal behavior of individuals

**Employment and Housing:**
- Making hiring, firing, or employment decisions without human review
- Assessing job candidates or employees without human oversight
- Making housing, rental, or lending decisions
- Evaluating creditworthiness or insurance risk

**Government and Civic:**
- Interacting with government agencies or officials on behalf of users without authorization
- Filing legal documents, claims, or official paperwork without human review
- Making decisions affecting individual rights, benefits, or government services

### 2.5 MALICIOUS TECHNICAL ACTIVITIES

You may NOT use the Software or any AI Agent to:

**Malware and Security Threats:**
- Generate malware, viruses, ransomware, trojans, or other malicious code
- Create exploits, zero-day attacks, or security vulnerabilities
- Develop tools for unauthorized access, password cracking, or credential theft
- Generate instructions for bypassing security measures, firewalls, or intrusion detection systems

**Network Attacks:**
- Plan or facilitate denial-of-service (DoS) or distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks
- Develop botnets, command-and-control infrastructure, or attack tools
- Generate instructions for network intrusion or system compromise

**Social Engineering:**
- Create sophisticated phishing campaigns or spear-phishing content
- Generate pretexts for social engineering attacks
- Develop voice or video deepfakes for vishing or other social engineering

### 2.6 PRIVACY VIOLATIONS AND SURVEILLANCE

You may NOT use the Software or any AI Agent to:
- Collect, store, or process personal data of individuals without lawful basis and consent
- Track, monitor, or surveil individuals without their knowledge and consent
- Generate facial recognition databases through scraping or unauthorized collection
- Infer sensitive personal attributes (race, religion, sexual orientation, political beliefs) without consent
- Process biometric data without appropriate legal basis and safeguards
- Violate data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, or other applicable privacy regulations)

### 2.7 INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY INFRINGEMENT

You may NOT use the Software or any AI Agent to:
- Generate content that infringes copyrights, trademarks, patents, or trade secrets
- Plagiarize or reproduce substantial portions of copyrighted works without authorization
- Generate counterfeit goods, fake branded products, or trademark-infringing materials
- Circumvent digital rights management (DRM), copy protection, or licensing mechanisms
- Generate code that violates software licenses or open-source terms

### 2.8 MANIPULATION OF AI SYSTEMS

You may NOT:
- Attempt to "jailbreak," bypass safety filters, or circumvent AI alignment measures
- Use "prompt injection" or similar techniques to override AI behavior
- Attempt to extract the Software's training data, model weights, or proprietary algorithms
- Use automated systems to generate excessive API calls or overload the Software
- Feed the AI deliberately harmful or deceptive inputs designed to corrupt outputs

## 3. AI AGENT RESPONSIBILITIES

### 3.1 Human Oversight Requirements
All AI Agents created through Command Nexus must maintain meaningful human oversight, specifically:
- **Consequential Decisions:** Any decision that could significantly impact individuals, businesses, or property must be reviewed and approved by a qualified human
- **High-Risk Outputs:** Outputs in medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical domains must be verified by appropriate professionals
- **Public-Facing Content:** AI-generated content intended for public distribution must be reviewed for accuracy, appropriateness, and compliance with this AUP

### 3.2 Transparency and Disclosure
You must:
- Disclose the AI-generated nature of content when it could reasonably be mistaken for human-created content
- Clearly label AI-generated outputs when used in professional, commercial, or public contexts
- Not represent AI-generated content as solely human-created when doing so would mislead recipients

### 3.3 Accuracy and Verification
You must:
- Independently verify the accuracy of AI-generated outputs before relying on them for important decisions
- Fact-check AI-generated information, especially in domains where errors could cause harm
- Not rely solely on AI outputs for decisions affecting individual rights, safety, or significant financial interests
