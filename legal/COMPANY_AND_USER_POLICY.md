## PART A: COMPANY POLICY (Pantheon Forge LLC)

### 1. MISSION STATEMENT

Pantheon Forge LLC builds local-first, privacy-first AI orchestration tools. We do not compromise on user data sovereignty, ethical AI guardrails, or transparent business practices.

### 2. CORE PRINCIPLES

| Principle | Commitment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Privacy** | No cloud data storage without explicit opt-in |
| **Transparency** | Clear pricing, no hidden fees, no dark patterns |
| **Security** | Anti-tamper by default, cryptographic license validation |
| **Ethics** | AI guardrails prevent harm, deception, or unauthorized actions |
| **Ownership** | Users own their AI configurations, Books, and outputs |

### 3. DEVELOPMENT STANDARDS

- All code changes require peer review
- Security-critical modules (license, tripwire, governance) require additional sign-off
- No telemetry without explicit user consent
- No backdoors, remote killswitches, or hidden functionality

### 4. SUPPORT & RESPONSE

| Issue Type | Response Time |
|------------|---------------|
| License / Activation | 24 hours |
| Critical Security | 4 hours |
| General Support | 48 hours |
| Feature Requests | 7 days (acknowledgment) |

### 5. REFUND POLICY

- **Trial:** Non-refundable ($10 is a one-time activation fee)
- **Subscriptions:** Prorated refund within 7 days if the Software has not been used
- **Voided licenses:** No refund — license voidance is permanent and irreversible

## PART B: USER POLICY (For End Users)

### 1. ACCEPTABLE USE

You may use Command Nexus to:
- Create, configure, and deploy AI agents for personal or business productivity
- Manage AI memory, guardrails, and capability profiles
- Orchestrate tasks, workflows, and document processing
- Export your own data and outputs

### 2. PROHIBITED USE

You may NOT use Command Nexus to:
- Develop competing AI orchestration platforms
- Train or fine-tune models on stolen or unauthorized data
- Generate disinformation, deepfakes, or harmful content
- Bypass license enforcement or AI limits through technical means
- Resell, share, or redistribute license keys
- Reverse engineer the Software for any purpose

### 3. AI GOVERNANCE & GUARDRAILS

All AI agents in Command Nexus operate under built-in governance:
- **Approval Gate:** High-risk actions require explicit user confirmation
- **Audit Logger:** All actions are logged locally for accountability
- **Capability Restrictions:** Each AI is bound to its declared use case
- **File Operation Gate:** No file writes without explicit approval

Users are responsible for:
- Reviewing AI-proposed actions before approval
- Ensuring AI outputs comply with applicable laws in their jurisdiction
- Not overriding guardrails to perform harmful actions

### 4. LICENSE KEY STEWARDSHIP

- Each license key is tied to the purchaser's email and device fingerprint
- Sharing keys violates this policy and voids the license
- Lost keys may be recovered by contacting support with purchase verification
- Keys found on piracy sites or forums are permanently blacklisted

### 5. CONSEQUENCES OF VIOLATION

| Violation | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| Sharing a key | Key voided, no refund |
| Reverse engineering | Key voided, legal action possible |
| Exceeding AI limits | Automatic enforcement, upgrade required |
| Harmful AI use | Account termination, law enforcement referral |
| Circumventing guards | Key voided, program may self-corrupt |

### 6. REPORTING VIOLATIONS

If you discover a security vulnerability, license leak, or policy violation:
- **Security:** security@pantheonforge.io
- **License Abuse:** abuse@pantheonforge.io
- **General:** support@pantheonforge.io

### 7. POLICY UPDATES

Pantheon Forge LLC may update this policy. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance. Material changes will be notified via email to active subscribers.
