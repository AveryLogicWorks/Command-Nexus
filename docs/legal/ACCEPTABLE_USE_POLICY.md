# Command Nexus — Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Last updated:** Draft

This policy describes permitted and prohibited uses of Command Nexus. Violations may result in suspension, termination of access, or legal action.

## Permitted Uses

You may use Command Nexus to:

- Manage, coordinate, and oversee AI-assisted workflows within your organization.
- Monitor and log activity for internal governance, compliance, and operational review.
- Integrate with approved tools and services for legitimate business purposes.

## Prohibited Uses

You may not use Command Nexus to:

- Generate, distribute, or facilitate illegal content or activity.
- Harass, threaten, or harm individuals or groups.
- Attempt to bypass, disable, or interfere with the software's safety or integrity systems.
- Misrepresent the software's outputs as human-generated when disclosure is required.
- Scrape, mine, or extract data in ways that violate third-party terms or privacy laws.
- Share access credentials with unauthorized individuals.

## Local-First Commitment

Command Nexus is designed to operate locally and keep sensitive data on your own systems. Do not configure it in ways that inadvertently expose internal data to unauthorized external services.

## Reporting Violations

If you become aware of a violation of this policy, report it to the licensee administrator or developer.

## Proprietary Notice

Command Nexus is custom proprietary software. It is not open source.
