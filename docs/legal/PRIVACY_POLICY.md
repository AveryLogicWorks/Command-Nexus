# Command Nexus — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** Draft

Command Nexus is built with a local-first, privacy-first design. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected.

## 1. Data Collection

By default, Command Nexus operates locally on your own hardware. The software does not:

- Transmit your content, prompts, or outputs to external servers without your explicit configuration.
- Collect personal information for marketing or advertising.
- Profile users for behavioral analytics.

## 2. Data You Control

You choose what data enters the system, including:

- AI configuration details and session notes.
- Workflow logs and audit records generated during use.
- Integration credentials for third-party services you connect.

This data remains under your control unless you explicitly configure external sync or export.

## 3. Local Storage

Data is stored locally in your designated workspace directory. You are responsible for:

- Securing the host device.
- Managing backups.
- Controlling who has access to the workspace.

## 4. Third-Party Integrations

If you connect Command Nexus to external APIs, models, or services, those interactions are governed by the third party's privacy policy. We encourage you to review those policies before connecting.

## 5. Security

The software includes defensive layers to help protect against unauthorized changes. However, no system is perfectly secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your environment.

## 6. Your Rights

Because the software runs locally, you retain full control over your data subject to your own policies and applicable law.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the software evolves. Review the latest version periodically.

## Proprietary Notice

Command Nexus is custom proprietary software. It is not open source.
