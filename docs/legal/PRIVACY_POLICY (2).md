# Command Nexus — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 2026 (Draft)
**Effective Date:** [To be configured]

Chad Harris / Pantheon Forge ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use Command Nexus ("Software").

**Our core principle: local-first, privacy-first.** By default, your data stays on your machine.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, password (if account creation is required).
- **AI Configurations:** Names, personality settings, capabilities, and instructions you create for your AI agents.
- **Book Content:** Memory entries, preferences, guardrails, and instructions you input through the Book AI dialog.
- **Support Communications:** Emails, chat logs, or feedback you send to us.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Crash Reports:** If you opt in, anonymized crash logs and error traces to help us fix bugs.
- **Usage Analytics:** If you opt in, anonymized feature usage statistics (e.g., "Forge opened 3 times"). No content, prompts, or outputs are included.
- **System Information:** OS version, app version, and hardware specs (for compatibility and debugging).

### 1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect (By Default)
We do **not** collect:
- Your prompts, conversations, or AI outputs.
- Files processed by the Software.
- Screen recordings or desktop activity.
- Personal information for marketing or advertising.
- Behavioral profiles or tracking cookies.

## 3. Local Storage and Your Control

By design, Command Nexus stores data **locally** on your device:
- **Workspace Directory:** `%USERPROFILE%\CommandNexusWorkspace\`
- **AI Saves:** JSON files you create and control.
- **Book Data:** Tree-structured markdown/Python exports.
- **Logs:** Local audit and activity logs.

**You are responsible for:**
- Securing the host device.
- Managing backups.
- Controlling physical and network access to the workspace.

---

## 4. Third-Party Integrations and AI Backends

The Software is an orchestration layer. If you connect to third-party AI services (OpenAI, Anthropic, local LLMs via Ollama, etc.):
- Those services receive your prompts and may store them under **their** privacy policies.
- We do not control, review, or moderate AI Backend data practices.
- **You are responsible for reviewing the privacy policy of any AI Backend you connect.**

---

## 5. Cookies and Tracking

The desktop Software does not use cookies.

If you visit our website:
- We may use essential cookies for site functionality.
- We do not use tracking cookies for advertising or behavioral profiling.
- You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- **Active Data:** Retained as long as you use the Software and do not delete it.
- **Account Information:** Retained until you delete your account or we terminate it.
- **Crash Reports/Analytics:** Retained for up to 90 days, then anonymized or deleted.
- **Deleted Data:** When you delete AI configurations or Book content, it is removed from your local storage. We do not retain copies unless you have configured cloud sync.

---

## 7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may disclose information only in the following circumstances:
- **With Your Consent:** When you explicitly authorize sharing.
- **Service Providers:** Trusted vendors who perform functions on our behalf (e.g., email delivery, crash analytics), bound by confidentiality agreements.
- **Legal Requirements:** To comply with a subpoena, court order, or other legal process.
- **Safety and Rights:** To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users or the public.
