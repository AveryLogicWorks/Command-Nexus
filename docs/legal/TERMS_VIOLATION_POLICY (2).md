# Command Nexus — Terms Violation Policy

**Last updated:** Draft

This policy explains how violations of the Terms of Use and Acceptable Use Policy are handled.

## 1. Detection

Violations may be detected through:

- Automated monitoring of anomalous or prohibited usage patterns.
- Reports from users, administrators, or external parties.
- Routine security and integrity scans.

## 2. Response Steps

When a potential violation is identified:

1. **Review:** The incident is reviewed to confirm whether a violation occurred.
2. **Notification:** If confirmed, the responsible party or administrator is notified.
3. **Remediation:** The user may be required to cease the violating activity and take corrective action.
4. **Enforcement:** Depending on severity, enforcement may include:
   - Warning and required retraining.
   - Temporary suspension of access.
   - Permanent termination of the license.
   - Referral to legal authorities if laws were broken.

## 3. Appeals

Users who believe a violation was incorrectly identified may appeal to the licensee administrator within a reasonable timeframe. Appeals are reviewed in good faith.

## 4. Record Keeping

Records of violations and responses are maintained for operational security and legal compliance, subject to the Privacy Policy and applicable data-retention laws.

## Proprietary Notice

Command Nexus is custom proprietary software. It is not open source.
