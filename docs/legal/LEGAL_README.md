### Step 1: Attorney Review
Hire a licensed attorney (preferably with software/IP experience) to review:
- EULA.md — ensure enforceability in your jurisdiction(s)
- Privacy Policy — ensure GDPR/CCPA/state-law compliance
- Terms of Use — ensure they match your actual business practices

**Estimated attorney time:** 3–6 hours for a standard software licensing review.

### Step 2: Customize
Replace all bracketed placeholders:
- `[To be configured]` — contact email, website URL, physical address
- `State of California` — change to your actual jurisdiction if different
- `Chad Harris / Pantheon Forge` — confirm entity name and structure

### Step 3: Register Trademarks
Before using ® symbol:
1. Conduct a USPTO trademark search for "Command Nexus" and "Pantheon Forge."
2. File intent-to-use or actual-use applications.
3. Consider international registration via Madrid Protocol if distributing globally.

### Step 4: Implement on Website
- Host Terms, Privacy, and AUP on their own pages (`/terms`, `/privacy`, `/aup`).
- Link to them from:
  - Footer of every page
  - Checkout/download flow
  - Account registration page
  - Email footers
- Require explicit checkbox consent for EULA during installation.

### Step 5: Maintain
- Update "Last updated" date on every revision.
- Notify users of material changes via email or in-app notice.
- Keep archived copies of prior versions for dispute resolution.

## Key Risk Areas for Legal Review

1. **Limitation of Liability** — The $100 cap in EULA §8 may be unenforceable in some jurisdictions. Some courts refuse to enforce caps on gross negligence or willful misconduct.

2. **Governing Law** — California was chosen as a default. If the owner resides elsewhere or incorporates elsewhere, this should match.

3. **Open Source Compliance** — Ensure all third-party licenses (PyQt6, Qt, etc.) are properly attributed and compatible with proprietary distribution.

4. **AI Output Liability** — As an AI orchestration tool, clarify that Licensor is not responsible for outputs generated by user-configured AI backends.

5. **Minors** — If the software could be used by minors, add COPPA-compliant provisions or an age-gate.

6. **Export Control** — If distributed internationally, ensure compliance with U.S. export control regulations (EAR/ITAR) regarding encryption and AI technology.
